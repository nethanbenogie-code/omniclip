How it works

From screen to slide in under a minute.

Start it

Double-click OmniClip.exe. It sits quietly in your system tray, always one keystroke away.

Capture

Press Ctrl+Shift+X and drag over what you want. Esc cancels.

Mark it up

Pick a tool, pick a colour, draw. Move or resize anything later with Select. Unlimited undo.

Share

Ctrl+C, then paste into PowerPoint, Word, Teams, Messenger or email. Or save as PNG / JPG.

Questions

 

Do I need to install it?

No. OmniClip is a single .exe file. Double-click it and it appears in your system tray.

What's the capture shortcut?

Ctrl+Shift+X captures a region from anywhere. Ctrl+Alt+R starts and stops a screen recording.

Does it upload my screenshots?

No. Nothing is uploaded. Captures stay on your computer until you paste or save them.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC.” Is that normal?

That message appears for new programs that aren't yet widely downloaded or code-signed. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Where are my files saved?

Images go to Pictures\OmniClip and recordings to Videos\OmniClip. Recent captures are also listed inside the app.
